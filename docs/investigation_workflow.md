# Illustrative alert investigation workflow

1. **Triage:** confirm data completeness, duplicates, timestamps, and rule explanation.
2. **Contextualize:** review account profile, expected activity, historical baseline, counterparty relationships and jurisdictional context (not available in this sample).
3. **Investigate:** distinguish legitimate explanations from unresolved concerns; document evidence and contradictions.
4. **Escalate:** refer unresolved alerts to a qualified compliance investigator under institutional procedures.
5. **Close and learn:** record disposition, quality issues, reviewer rationale and feedback for future calibration.

## Example synthetic finding
Transactions T002–T004 are three outbound transfers between $9,000 and $10,000 within 20 minutes for the same synthetic account. The demo flags this pattern for review; it **does not** conclude structuring, criminal intent or a regulatory reporting obligation.

Transaction T007 triggers the illustrative large-transaction rule. A high transaction amount alone is not evidence of money laundering.

## Limitations
No real customers, sanctions data, KYC files, beneficial ownership information, transaction graph, or validated jurisdiction-specific requirements are used. No real suspicious activity reports are generated.
